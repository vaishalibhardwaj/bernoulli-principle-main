## Direction contract

THESIS: A recon tool that feels like a friendly consumer money app: soft pastel fields that say what kind of thing you are looking at, so the dense admin grid of grey cards the category ships is refused.

OWN-WORLD: Pale lilac-grey canvas; white 28-32px rounded cards; lilac (#d9c9fb) and pink (#f6b4d6) colour fields owning whole promo and status cards; violet (#7b61e8) pill primary buttons; black circular icon buttons; green for gains/active, pink-red for negatives; yellow highlight. Urbanist, heavy and tight for headings and numbers. Glossy 3D clay objects (coins, cubes, gears, folders, clocks).

STORY: You land, see what needs you in coloured cards, and act in one click.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Big bold greeting or screen title top-left, black round icon actions top-right, then a row of colour-field cards (lilac, pink) carrying the screen's key counts with a clay object breaking the card corner; lists follow on white.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) — "redesign the recon application with the exact same aesthetic as above" (user's reference image of a pastel fintech app). Signature interaction: cards lift and their clay object tilts on hover.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
